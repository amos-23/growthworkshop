Questions before you register

Is this relevant if I'm not a marketer?

Yes, if growth is part of your job or your business. The workshop is for founders and business owners, marketers, growth professionals and people exploring how AI can support growth.

Is this a workshop about AI tools?

No. The focus is growth decisions and customer acquisition. AI is covered as a way to research, plan, analyse and execute more effectively, not as the product.

What's the difference between the two tickets?

Standard Access gives you the full live workshop. Growth Intensive includes everything in Standard Access, plus a private one-on-one growth strategy session with Amos, personalised discussion of your acquisition challenges and tailored guidance on potential next steps.

Is the limit on Growth Intensive real?

Yes. Growth Intensive is capped at 10 participants because it includes a private session with Amos.

When is it, and how do I register?
