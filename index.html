The problem

More marketing activity doesn't automatically mean more revenue.

You are publishing content, running campaigns, testing channels and trying new tools. But are those activities actually helping your business grow?

Traffic, impressions and engagement can look impressive on a report without translating into enough customers or revenue.

The challenge is knowing where your biggest opportunities are, what is holding back growth, which activities deserve your resources and how to connect marketing decisions to commercial results.

This workshop will help you develop a more deliberate approach to finding growth opportunities and improving customer acquisition.

What the activity looks like

  • Content published
  • Campaigns running
  • Channels being tested
  • New tools adopted
  • Traffic, impressions, engagement
?

What the business needs to know

  • Are we acquiring the right customers?
  • Where is growth being held back?
  • What deserves our time and budget?
  • Is this contributing to revenue?

Activity metrics have their place. The question is whether they connect to customers and revenue.

The outcome

Make better growth decisions. Acquire customers more effectively. Work towards more revenue.

Growth rarely comes from doing everything at once. It comes from understanding your business, identifying the right opportunities, prioritising the work that matters and improving your execution.

At the workshop, participants will learn a structured way to approach these decisions and understand how AI can support the work.

The workshop

Improve customer acquisition
